# RootRoads Privacy Policy

_Last updated: 6 November 2026_

RootRoads is a community directory and partners app for Root. This policy explains what RootRoads
uses, why, and how long it is kept. RootRoads has no ads and no tracking.

## What stays inside your community

RootRoads runs inside each Root community that installs it. In that community's own app storage it
keeps the listing the owner wrote, the list of partners, partner requests, the partner and
advertising settings, and, for the open advertising channel, each member's Root user ID with the
time of their last ad (deleted after about a week).

## What RootRoads shares outside your community

Only if the owner or a manager lists the community. RootRoads then sends to the RootRoads/OmniRoot
relay, so other communities using RootRoads or OmniRoot can see it: the community's name, picture,
member count, and the tagline, description, tags, language, age label, owner display name and
invite link that the owner typed in. The community is identified only by one-way hashes of its id.
Partner requests (the community's name and the note written with the request), answers to them and
listing reports (a short reason) are stored on the relay so the other community can read them.

Listings are removed when the owner unlists the community, are hidden after 7 days without an
update and deleted after 30 days. Partner requests expire after 14 days.

No member data is shared: the only person named is the owner name the owner chose to show.

## What RootRoads does not store

No messages (posts in the open advertising channel are checked and not kept), voice or files, no
email addresses, passwords, payment details or real names, no location or device information.

## Data kept on your own device

Your language choice is remembered in your Root client's local storage.

## How long data is kept

See above. If a community removes RootRoads, Root deletes the app's data for that community; its
listing then drops out of the directory within 30 days, or right away if the owner unlists it first.
